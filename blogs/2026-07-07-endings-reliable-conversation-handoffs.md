Lorikeet's Endings feature lets AI support agents reliably execute conversation closures by defining named, specific endpoints with predetermined action sequences. Rather than leaving handoffs to chance, the approach improves escalation reliability and gives teams measurable insight into how conversations actually conclude across thousands of interactions.
