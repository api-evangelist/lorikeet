Auto QA automatically scores every support ticket against company policies, catching roughly 99.7% of problematic responses. Unlike traditional sampling that misses issues in unreviewed tickets, this system provides comprehensive quality assurance from inside the support platform, with access to relevant SOPs and policies, keeping standards consistent across both AI and human agents.
